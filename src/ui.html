Live activity

    Why doesn't a button show up?

    Some buttons never reach your computer as a normal button: they're handled inside the mouse (like the MX Master's wheel-mode button) or only talk to the maker's own app. If Logi Options+, G HUB or Razer Synapse is running, it can take buttons before unimouse sees them.

    On Windows, only the middle, back and forward buttons are visible to unimouse for now.

    General

    Start at loginRun unimouse in the background whenever you log in.
    Press to selectPressing a button on your mouse selects it in the Buttons tab.
    Config file

    Everything here is saved to that file as you go. You can also edit it by hand, and copy it to another computer.

    Apps

    An app matches by its name on macOS, its .exe name on Windows or its window class on Linux. List several names with |, and end one with * to match any version, e.g. adobe photoshop*.
